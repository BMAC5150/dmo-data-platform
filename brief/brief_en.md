# Business events brief, October 2026
SYNTHETIC DATA. Prototype: hotels, groups and figures are invented.

## Month performance
[TABLA]
October was a month of lost ground in groups. The destination filled more rooms than a year ago (occupancy of 52.4%, 4.9 points higher), but group room nights fell 4.8%: the month's growth did not come from groups. Demand is there: 12 leads came in, 50.0% more than in October 2025, and 81 so far this year, 30.6% more; year to date, group room nights are still up 1.4%. The director is asked to close Grupo Financiero Cumbres this week, which arrives on November 18, 2026, and to decide how to respond on air access, the loss reason that costs us the most room nights.

## Three findings
1. **Air access costs us more room nights than price.** Price is the most frequent reason (17 groups lost), but air access cost 8,913 estimated room nights across 14 groups, against 6,640 for price. Puerto Vallarta beat us on air access 4 times, for 3,399 estimated room nights.
2. **Hotels bring us the groups we actually win.** Hotel referrals win 40.0% (8 of 20); Cvent RFPs win 15.4% (2 of 13). Mexican corporate is the strongest sector, at 39.1% (9 of 23); sports lost all 10 decided.
3. **September 2027 holds most of the pipeline.** It is the quietest month (41.2% occupancy last year) and has 6 priority prospects with 4,518 estimated room nights. The largest prospects in the pipeline (Corporativo Cedro, Laboratorios Sauce, Seguros Altavista and Grupo Cumbres) all arrive that month, between September 9 and 27.

## Three actions for November 2026
1. **Close Grupo Financiero Cumbres.** Owner: Director · Deadline: before November 18, 2026 · At stake: 657 estimated room nights. It has been open 163 days: confirm the proposal or release the rooms. Sales also reviews the 10 leads open for more than 90 days and confirms or closes each one by November 30, 2026.
2. **Secure September 2027 capacity with partner hotels for Corporativo Cedro, Laboratorios Sauce, Seguros Altavista and Grupo Cumbres.** Owner: Sales with partner hotels · Deadline: November 30, 2026 · At stake: 1,458 estimated room nights at Corporativo Cedro, 912 at Laboratorios Sauce, 872 at Seguros Altavista and 662 at Grupo Cumbres. Ask for tentative holds before quoting, so we do not compete with ourselves for the same dates.
3. **Take the groups we lost on air access to the airlines.** Owner: Director · Deadline: November 30, 2026 · At stake: 8,913 estimated room nights lost in 12 months. Sales lists the 14 groups with their home cities; the director presents the list to the airlines as real demand for routes and schedules.
